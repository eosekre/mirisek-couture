Mirisek Couture blends African textile heritage with modern luxury fashion. We design bespoke garments, train emerging fashion talent, and celebrate craftsmanship through every stitch.

Our Philosophy

Luxury rooted in African identity.

Mirisek Couture honours the beauty of African textiles including Kente, Ankara, Adire and Bogolan mud cloth. Each garment is crafted to express elegance, culture and individuality.

Through Mirisek Couture Academy, we also train new designers in sewing, garment construction, pattern drafting, finishing and fashion entrepreneurship.
