Our Story

Luxury rooted in African identity.

Mirisek Couture is a fashion house dedicated to refined African luxury, bespoke bridal wear and beautifully finished couture garments.

Through Mirisek Couture Academy, we train aspiring designers to master sewing, garment finishing, pattern drafting and the discipline of professional fashion production.
